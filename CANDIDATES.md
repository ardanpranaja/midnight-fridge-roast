# Candidates

Alternate lines considered for "Midnight Fridge Roast". The chosen line won because it aims at the fridge's own petty rule — the appliance gets a bedtime, the hungry human doesn't — in a single tight beat that sounds like something said out loud at the door.

1. "My fridge locks itself at midnight like it pays the electric bill and I just live here rent-free." — Good turn, but the bill-payer joke drifts from the midnight refusal itself into a generic roommate gag.
2. "My smart fridge won't open after midnight — it says it's 'resting', like the leftovers inside aren't on a deadline." — Funny image, but it needs a dash and a quoted excuse to work; too many moving parts for one line.
3. "At midnight my fridge goes on strike, guarding the cheese like it signed a union contract." — The cheese image is vivid, but "on strike" is a second joke crowding the refusal, and the fridge isn't really striking — it's enforcing a curfew.
4. "My fridge locks at midnight to 'protect my health' — bold of an appliance that keeps ice cream at arm's reach all day." — Close second: the health-versus-ice-cream turn is sharp, but the line is longer and leans on the fridge's excuse rather than the refusal itself.
5. "The fridge treats midnight like a curfew, as if the pickles inside are underage." — The curfew frame is right, but the pickles punchline is a throwaway; the joke lands on the pickles, not on the person locked out.

Chosen: "The fridge has a bedtime now, and apparently I'm the only one in this house who doesn't."
